Website privacy notice.

A small footprint. A clear privacy notice.

This landing page has no analytics, advertising pixels or embedded social feeds. Its code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Copy buttons write only the displayed command to your clipboard, only when clicked.

Hosting, linked pages & contacting us

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Requests necessarily reach the host, which may process IP addresses and technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not control GitHub's hosting logs or their retention.

Social links are ordinary links, not embeds. Following them opens a service with its own privacy practices. The documentation can store display preferences locally and include external resources; benchmark pages currently load Chart.js from jsDelivr. The no-tracking description above applies to this landing page, not to every linked service.

If you email officialrullst@gmail.com, the team and its email provider receive your address and message to handle your inquiry. Don't include credentials or unnecessary personal data. Contact that address about privacy questions or requests; public issues and social posts are not private support channels.

Last reviewed: September 5, 2026. This notice describes the website—not data processing in applications built with Rullst—and does not claim universal legal compliance.
